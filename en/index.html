Installation

Up and running in three steps

All you need is a Linux server or VM with Docker – 1 GB of RAM and 10 GB of storage are enough.

Download

Download the ZIP and extract it on your server. It is deliberately small: it only contains the configuration and instructions, Docker downloads NetDoc itself on first start.

unzip netdoc-v*.zip -d /opt

Start

One command pulls NetDoc and starts it together with its database.

cd /opt/netdoc
docker compose up -d

Set up

Open it in your browser; the wizard asks for your company name, an admin account and your license. Done.

http://your-server

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a device?

Counts: every device you add to NetDoc – for example switches, routers, firewalls, servers, PCs, laptops, printers and access points.

Doesn’t count: patch panels, wall outlets, cables, racks, software, licenses and contracts – you can add as many of those as you like.

What happens when I reach the limit?

Everything stays readable and editable – you just can’t add new devices. Enter a license key for a larger tier and you can carry on right away, no reinstall needed.

What happens after the 12 months of updates?

Your installed version keeps running indefinitely, without any restrictions. You only need to renew updates if you want newer versions.

Can I upgrade to a larger tier later?

Yes. You only pay the difference to the larger tier: send us a short email and you’ll get a personal coupon code, and after the purchase a new license key that replaces your old one.

How do I get my license key?

NetDoc itself is always free to download. After your purchase, you receive your personal license key by email, usually within 24 hours. Enter it in the setup wizard or later under Settings → License.

What do I need for the installation?

A Linux server or VM with Docker and Docker Compose (tested with Ubuntu and Debian), 1 GB of RAM and 10 GB of storage. Runs on Intel/AMD and ARM. An internet connection is only needed for downloading and for updates.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your server. NetDoc doesn’t send any data to us or to third parties. Passwords and credentials are stored encrypted.

How do updates work?

With two commands: docker compose pull and docker compose up -d. NetDoc applies database changes automatically on startup. Creating a backup beforehand takes one click under Settings → Backup.

Is there support?

We help with installation questions and bugs by email at support@getnetdoc.com – on a best-effort basis, without guaranteed response times. Detailed instructions are included with every download.

Can I try NetDoc first?

Yes – the free version includes every feature and is free forever for up to 25 devices. If you like it, just enter a license key; your data stays as it is.

Can I get a refund?

If NetDoc isn’t right for you, we refund the purchase price within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. A short email is all it takes.
