NetDoc itself is available for free download on this website at any time. What you purchase is the license key: we send it by email to the address you provided at checkout, usually within 24 hours.

You can upgrade to a larger tier at any time and only pay the difference to the price of the new tier. You receive a personal coupon code for this and, after the purchase, a new license key that replaces your previous one. Just write to support@getnetdoc.com.

5. Refunds

If NetDoc isn’t right for you, we refund the purchase price within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. A short email is all it takes. The refund terminates the license; afterwards, NetDoc may only be used within the free tier (up to 25 devices).
